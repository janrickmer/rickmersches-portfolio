# Surface brief: index.html (Das Rickmersche Portfolio)

Scope: the public one-page documentation (`index.html`), plus `impressum.html` inheriting its world.
Visitor mode: **Read** — the visitor came to understand the strategy; the calculator follows understanding.
Audience: interested private investors who want to rebuild the plan with their own monthly amount.
Constraints: facts, numbers, ISINs, notes and body copy stay verbatim; headings and labels may be rewritten.
No third-party requests; fonts embedded; pictures and the one video self-hosted and credited.
Legacy anchors `#tool1` / `#tool2` keep working. Light + dark, print, reduced motion, forced colours.
Memorable moment: the visitor's own monthly amount, split live across regions, the map and the 16 positions.
Unresolved: none.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The category standard played straight — a clear, trustworthy German finance documentation at the
craft level of Finanztip (readable explanations, clear structure) and Trade Republic (large type, few
elements, strong pictures). It refuses both the cream-and-gold private-bank brochure it replaces and the
dashboard of stat tiles and icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: White ground, one cool surface step (#F2F5F9), ink #0E1726, one finance blue (#1458D6) for the
action and the chart's lead series, calm blue/teal tints for regions, a muted gold only where gold is
meant. Inter, one family, optical sizes. Flat fields instead of lines; full-bleed blue and ink pages;
square photographs; round buttons.

STORY: The visitor learns what the plan is, sees where each euro of their own amount goes (regions, map,
positions), understands why (regions as controls, German anchor, gold, no rebalancing), then converts
their amount in the calculator and leaves knowing it is not advice.

FIRST VIEWPORT: As the approved sketch `d-kanon`: plain top bar (wordmark left, five links, round blue
action). Left ~55 %: the title on two lines at display size, the supporting sentence, the action plus
one text link, the five key facts as large numerals with sentence-case captions. Right ~45 %: one filled
surface panel with the region split as a stacked bar, legend with values, and the largest countries —
made live by the visitor's monthly amount.

FORM: canon (the standing exit chosen by the user over the assigned roll), executed at full fidelity;
composition habits from design-taste-frontend "The printed annual" (fixed text axis, alternating text
pages and full-bleed colour pages). seed: ac604b55dcb3401a
